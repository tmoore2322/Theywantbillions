THEY WANT BILLIONS

A Colony Survival Strategy

They don’t want your walls. They want the budget.
Survive the fiscal year.

The loop

  • Day 1 is quiet. Build cottages, a farm, a sawmill; wall the ridge gaps. Research at the Town Hall (T).
  • A Wave arrives at the start of every day from Day 2. Day 16 is the big one.
  • A building at 0 HP is not rubble: it is Occupied. It stops paying you, spawns Activists, drains the Treasury and flips its neighbors faster. Stand a unit next to it to recapture.
  • Seizure Crews nationalize industry. Agitprop Vans paint. Goons jump walls. Tankies break gates. Nevertheless persists. Thicc Support heals from a scooter.
  • From Day 10, lieutenants: Sernie Banders vacuums the Treasury, Liz Barren regulates your repairs, Alex Occasion-Cortex shuts your mills.
  • Militia are cheap and cost gold every second. Dismiss them between waves.
  • Lose: Town Hall falls, or the Treasury sits at zero for 45s. Win: survive Day 16.

Controls

  • Space pause / resume (command while paused)
  • WASD / arrows / middle-drag: pan · wheel: zoom · H home
  • Z X C V tray tabs · 1–9 pick · click to place (drag for walls) · right-click / Esc cancel
  • Left-click select · drag box · right-click: move / attack / recapture / retrieve
  • M muster militia (Shift+M ×5) · K dismiss · N train Minuteman · T tech
  • F select militia · G select army · R repair · Del demolish · L war levy
  • F5 save · F9 load · ☰ menu (new map, seed, difficulty)

Layout doctrine

  • Town Hall center; Treasury touching it, never on a wall.
  • Homes in a dense inner block one road off the wall. A house on the wall is a spawn node.
  • Sawmill, quarry, mine and workshop in a walled pocket with an inward gate. Farms behind the curtain.
  • Militia Hall beside the main gate. Chapel on the housing side. School inside the court radius, off the wall.
  • Print Shop and Deed Office in the inner ring: Goons hunt them.
  • Gates are weapons: stakes and a killbox in front of the gate with towers on both sides.
  • A fat Treasury raises Momentum and calls a bigger Wave. Chapels bleed Momentum.

CHARTER OF THE TOWN — Tech

One research at a time, at the Town Hall. Guns-only dies. Paper-only gets walked through.
